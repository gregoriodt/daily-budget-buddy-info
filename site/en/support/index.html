Support — Daily Budget Buddy for iPhone

Updated November 16, 2026 — standalone iPhone profile. This page covers the iPhone version without app accounts, separately from earlier web/cloud versions.

For help, feedback and data requests, email gregorio.ditrapani@icloud.com. Operator: Gregorio Di Trapani, Italy. Contact is optional and requires no GitHub account. No technical-support response time is guaranteed.

What to include

App version/build, iOS version, language, intended action and steps to reproduce the issue with invented data. Do not send passwords, banking details, budget exports or personal screenshots. Recreate a case with synthetic data if an image is useful. Personal-data requests are handled separately from technical bug reports.

Where your data is

The standalone version stores budgets on this iPhone and requires no sign-in. An initially empty archive does not mean that earlier cloud budgets were deleted: they are not transferred automatically. Your Apple credentials are not app login credentials.

The financial archive is excluded from automatic iOS backup. In Settings → Device data and backup, save a manual copy through Files. The JSON file is readable in plain text; keep it safe. Do not rely on the internal previous copy alone: it cannot be selected as a restore source in the interface.

If the archive will not open

Unlock the iPhone and try again. Do not uninstall the app to troubleshoot an archive error: you could lose the archive. If you have a compatible backup created by this version, select it in Settings, inspect the preview and decide whether to replace current data. A readable damaged archive may allow explicit recovery, but a valid backup is still required. Recovery without a backup or after losing the device is not promised.

Widgets and reminders

Widgets need a local budget and summaries prepared by the app. iOS controls updates; after the 31-day horizon, reopen the app. Lock Screen amounts are initially hidden. Notifications are local and optional; check permission in iOS Settings. Completing a reminder does not record an expense.

Privacy and support handling

The privacy policy covers the archive, copies, widgets, support and any visits to the information pages online. Email is handled manually in the operator’s iCloud mailbox, with deletion within 90 days after resolution of the request, except for messages necessary for applicable legal duties or concrete disputes. Longer retention is limited to the time required by that duty or the protection of the rights involved. This is not automated deletion by the app or from providers’ backups. Private GitHub technical tickets contain only cases reproducible with invented data: we do not copy senders, personal conversations or attachments into them.
