Privacy — Daily Budget Buddy for iPhone

Updated November 16, 2026 — standalone iPhone profile. This policy describes the iPhone version without app accounts and does not replace the policy for earlier web/cloud versions.

Operator and contact

The service is operated by Gregorio Di Trapani, Italy. Support and data requests: gregorio.ditrapani@icloud.com. Gregorio Di Trapani is the controller for support correspondence.

Data on your device

Budgets, amounts, currencies, dates, transactions, debts/credits, any person labels and notes, reminders and annual plans are entered manually and kept in the app’s private archive on this iPhone. A person label is required for debts/credits; an alias is sufficient. The app does not require real names, Contacts access, banking credentials or card numbers.

This profile requires no app account and does not transmit budgets to a server operated by us. It contains no advertising or usage-analytics tools and has no bank connections. Earlier cloud records remain separate: they are not automatically imported, changed or deleted.

Language, preferred currency, the introductory notice and widget preferences are stored locally, separately from the financial backup. Archive protection uses iOS facilities; it is not password encryption configured within the app.

Backups, restoration and local retention

The financial archive and the internal copies stored with it are excluded from automatic iOS backup. You can export a manual copy through Files. This JSON file is plain text: anyone with the file can read it. Selecting an iCloud or other provider location is your choice and involves that service’s data handling.

Restoration requires a compatible backup, a preview and confirmation, and replaces current local data. An ordinary restore retains a private previous copy; a later restore may replace that copy. Recovery from a damaged archive preserves readable originals separately. Damaged originals are not automatically deleted. These internal copies have no restore or undo command in the interface and cannot guarantee recovery of missing data.

The export flow attempts to remove temporary files, including on a subsequent launch or export. Failed cleanup may leave residual files until a later successful attempt. This does not delete copies you saved elsewhere. Data remains in the installation’s container while present; deleting individual transactions is not secure erasure of every previous copy. Removing the app or losing the iPhone can lose the archive. Removing the app does not delete external exports or copies already made by system services.

Widgets and reminders

The app and its widgets share summaries on the same device: budget identifier and revision, period, currency, amounts and daily counts, for up to 31 precomputed days. Widget files do not contain individual transactions, notes or person labels. Widget cache, preferences and the notification opt-in preference may be included in iOS-managed backups; reminder financial records remain in the archive excluded from automatic backup. Lock Screen amounts are initially hidden; you can change visibility. This choice affects display and does not remove amounts from widget files. iOS controls when updates appear.

Notifications are local and optional. Their wording is generic and contains no amounts, notes or names. Permission can be changed in iOS Settings. These functions do not send a budget to our server.

If you contact support

Email is a separate choice and is not required to use the budget. The app opens your email program only when you choose, without automatically attaching budget data. If you send a message, we receive your address, any sender name and whatever you choose to write or attach, to respond and resolve issues. Avoid passwords, bank statements, financial backups and screenshots containing real data. Not sending email does not restrict app functions, although missing information may prevent us from answering a particular request.

Correspondence is handled manually in the operator’s iCloud mailbox through the email services involved. A technical issue may be tracked in the private GitHub repository using invented reproduction data only; we do not copy senders, personal conversations or attachments into that tracker. Support emails are not used for advertising or profiling.

For support necessary to perform a contract with you, the legal basis is GDPR Article 6(1)(b). For general questions or feedback, we process necessary information under the legitimate interest in responding and improving the app’s operation, Article 6(1)(f), taking your rights and reasonable expectations into account. Handling requests to exercise your rights and meeting applicable legal duties rely on Article 6(1)(c). A concrete dispute may require limited retention under the legitimate interest in defending rights, Article 6(1)(f). You may object to processing based on legitimate interest as provided by the GDPR.

The operator manually deletes conversations within 90 days after resolution of the request. Only messages necessary for an applicable legal duty or a concrete dispute are retained separately for longer, for the time required by that duty or the protection of the rights involved. The 90-day rule is not an app timer or a promise of immediate deletion from providers’ backup systems.

Email services and transfers

Delivery and storage of correspondence involve your email provider and Apple for the operator’s iCloud mailbox, under their respective services. The iCloud terms identify Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland, as the contractual provider for Italy. Apple’s privacy policy describes recipients, providers and retention according to service purposes. It provides for international processing, including in the United States, and standard contractual clauses for transfers of data collected in the European Economic Area. You can request a copy of the clauses through Apple’s privacy contacts. We do not guarantee that all email stays in Italy or that providers delete every copy within our manual deadline.

If you visit the information pages online

Reading the policy included in the app does not require a connection. If you view a copy of these pages on GitHub Pages, your browser contacts the hosting service: GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security even without account sign-in, as described in the Pages documentation. The information pages contain no collection forms, analytics or advertising tools; this does not exclude technical processing by the provider.

GitHub’s privacy statement describes processing for which GitHub is the controller, purposes, retention tied to service needs and international transfers, including to the United States, with the stated safeguards, including standard contractual clauses. Contacts for information and rights are in the same statement. Visiting the site or opening other external links is optional and separate from using the budget offline.

Choices and rights

For data kept only on your device, use the app’s controls and manual backups; the operator has no copy to inspect or change remotely. For support data we receive, contact us to exercise applicable access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection rights. See the Italian data protection authority’s rights information. You may lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority.

Apple settings for diagnostics, App Store, TestFlight and backups depend on system services and user choices; they are not an analytics system introduced by this app. See Apple’s privacy policy and the terms of your chosen email or file provider.
